**LLM assessment**: Does not read as obvious AI slop at first glance. Real Piura street references, a 4-star review mixed with 5-star ones, and concrete service copy avoid the generic-AI-copy trap. #### Overall Impression

The visual system is in good shape after this session's blue/gold cleanup, but the page has three real-world trust bugs that matter more than any styling issue for a business whose entire pitch is "call us, we're reliable": the location the copy displays isn't the location the map links to, the "24/7" promise contradicts the posted hours, and mobile users (the majority audience per PRODUCT.md) cannot open the nav menu at all. Fix those before spending more time on visual polish.

#### What's Working

- **The Signal Blue / dark console system reads cohesive**, not templated, now that gold and gradient text are gone — it matches DESIGN.md's "Emergency Dispatch Console" north star.
- **Real, specific copy**: Piura street references, a mixed 4/5-star review set, and concrete per-service descriptions avoid generic AI-landing-page language.
- **CTA discipline**: the WhatsApp/call action is reachable in the first screen and repeats at every natural decision point (hero, each service card, footer, floating button) without ever feeling like a wall of choices.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P0] Displayed address doesn't match the map/directions link**: `constants.ts` shows `address: 'Av. Loreto, Piura'` in the Location card, but `mapQuery: 'Av. Country 564, Piura'` drives both the embedded map and the "Cómo llegar" button.
  **Why it matters**: for an emergency service, sending a stressed visitor to the wrong street address at 2am is the single worst thing this page could do; it directly contradicts PRODUCT.md's "earn trust fast" principle.
  **Fix**: reconcile `address` and `mapQuery` in `constants.ts` to the same real street.
  **Suggested command**: /impeccable harden

- **[P0] Mobile menu button is non-functional**: the hamburger button in `Header.tsx` has no `onClick`, no state, and no menu is ever rendered; the four nav links (`Servicios`, `Equipo`, `Recomendaciones`, `Ubicación`) live only in the `hidden md:flex` desktop nav.
  **Why it matters**: PRODUCT.md names mobile search as the primary access path; on mobile today, only the two hero CTAs, the header phone icon, and the floating WhatsApp button are reachable without manually scrolling the entire page. All the trust-building content (team, reviews, location) is effectively unreachable via navigation.
  **Fix**: wire the button to open a mobile menu (sheet or dropdown) with the same four links.
  **Suggested command**: /impeccable adapt

- **[P1] "24/7" promise contradicts posted hours**: the hero H1, the pulsing badge, and the footer all claim 24/7 availability, but the Location section prints "Lun - Dom: 8:30am - 7:00pm".
  **Why it matters**: this is heuristic #2 (match with the real world) broken at the exact moment of highest stakes; a visitor locked out at 3am who reads the hours will assume no one answers.
  **Fix**: either state explicitly that the emergency line is always staffed while the physical taller keeps 8:30-7 hours, or drop the 24/7 claim if it isn't true.
  **Suggested command**: /impeccable clarify

- **[P1] Google review link is a broken placeholder**: `googleReviewUrl` in `constants.ts` still contains `TU_PLACE_ID_AQUI`, so "Dejar una Recomendación" opens a dead search URL in production.
  **Why it matters**: a visitor who just read glowing reviews and wants to verify or add one hits a broken link, undermining the exact credibility the Recommendations section is built to establish.
  **Fix**: replace with the real Google Place ID.
  **Suggested command**: /impeccable harden

- **[P2] Fake "live" decoration reads as borrowed SaaS language**: the hero's terminal-style mock panel ("SERVICIO 24/7 ACTIVO / OPERATIVO", traffic-light window dots) and the WhatsApp button's permanent "1" unread badge are dashboard/inbox conventions with no real function here.
  **Why it matters**: PRODUCT.md's anti-references explicitly reject anything that "reads as a cheap franchise site" rather than a real team; decorative fake status has the opposite intent (looks technical) but the same effect (feels staged) once a visitor notices the badge count never changes.
  **Fix**: replace with something concretely true (e.g. real response-time stat) or remove.
  **Suggested command**: /impeccable clarify

- **[P2] Repeated icon-title-text unit**: the Team section stacks three icon-in-circle + heading + one-liner rows, and the Services grid uses four near-identical icon cards; together they lean on the same generic unit more than the "solid and direct" component philosophy in DESIGN.md wants.
  **Fix**: vary the Team section's layout away from the same card unit used in Services.
  **Suggested command**: /impeccable layout

#### Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (First-Timer)**: Reads "24 HORAS" and "OPERATIVO" at the top of the page and expects a real 24/7 line, then would hit the conflicting 8:30am-7:00pm hours in the Location card further down — enough to make Jordan hesitate to call outside those hours, exactly when this service exists to be called. Also easy to miss the location entirely: the map is click-to-load ("Cargar Mapa Interactivo") with no default preview, so a first-timer who doesn't notice the button never sees where the business is.

**Riley (Stress Tester)**: Would immediately catch that the Location card's printed address (Av. Loreto) doesn't match where "Cómo llegar" and the embedded map actually point (Av. Country 564) — a textbook promise-vs-reality gap. Would also click "Dejar una Recomendación" and land on a broken placeholder Google Maps search URL.

**Casey (Distracted Mobile User)**: Taps the hamburger icon and nothing happens — no menu opens, no error, just silence. With no way to reach Servicios/Equipo/Recomendaciones/Ubicación except manual scrolling, Casey (one-handed, interrupted, low patience) likely bounces after the hero unless the two visible CTAs already answer the need. The tel:/wa.me deep links that *are* reachable work well for Casey — that part is a genuine win.

#### Minor Observations

- Three different places (hero badge, floating WhatsApp button, Location map markers) reuse the same ping/pulse animation for three unrelated meanings (live status, unread count, map location), diluting what "pulse" signals anywhere on the page.
- Footer copyright reads "© 2024 Cerrajería Piura" — stale relative to the current date, a small freshness/trust signal for a repeat visitor.
- The hero mock panel's red/yellow/green traffic-light dots are a macOS window-chrome convention with no function here; harmless but adds visual noise without informational payoff.
