# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People in Piura, Peru who need a locksmith, often in a stressful, time-sensitive moment: locked out of their home or car, a broken key, a lock that failed, or a security upgrade they've been putting off. Many arrive via a quick mobile search at odd hours (late night, early morning) and decide within seconds whether to call. A smaller segment browses proactively for smart security upgrades (duplicate keys, smart locks) when not in a crisis.

## Product Purpose

24/7 Piura - Cerrajería Experta is a landing page for a professional locksmith service. It exists to convert a stressed, time-pressured visitor into a phone call or WhatsApp message as fast as possible, while also establishing enough credibility (team, service area, reviews) that the visitor trusts a stranger to handle their home or car security. Success is a fast, confident contact action, not a long browse session.

## Brand Personality

Urgent and reliable. The voice is direct and competent: it acknowledges the visitor's urgency without dramatizing it, and answers the two questions on their mind immediately — "can you come now" and "can I trust you." Not folksy or over-familiar, not cold or corporate. Think emergency-service composure: calm competence under pressure.

## Anti-references

Generic template-locksmith sites: stock photos of keys and padlocks, gold-and-black "security company" clichés, cluttered service lists with no hierarchy, walls of SEO keyword text, fake urgency banners ("¡OFERTA POR TIEMPO LIMITADO!"). Avoid anything that reads as a cheap franchise site rather than a real, competent local team.

## Design Principles

- Answer "can you come now" before anything else — the primary call-to-action (call/WhatsApp) must be reachable within one glance on any screen.
- Composure over drama — urgency is conveyed through clarity and speed of access, not alarming colors, countdowns, or exclamation-heavy copy.
- Earn trust fast — team, service area, and real reviews carry more weight here than feature lists; surface them early and make them feel concrete, not templated.
- One decision at a time — a stressed visitor should never have to parse a wall of options; each section makes one thing obvious to do next.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Standard best practices: sufficient color contrast in both light and dark themes, legible body text, clear focus states, no reliance on color alone to convey urgency or status.
